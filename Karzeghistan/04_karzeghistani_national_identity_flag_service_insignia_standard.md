# 1. Purpose

This document establishes a coherent national visual identity for contemporary Karzeghistan.

It defines the art-direction basis for:

- the national flag;
- government symbolism;
- Karzeghistani Defence Forces;
- Karzeghistani National Police;
- Karzeghistani Border Guard;
- aircraft markings;
- vehicle markings;
- shoulder patches;
- government buildings;
- editor icons;
- future state props.

The objective is to create a recognisable Armaverse country identity without copying a real national flag or inventing unnecessary political lore.

---

# PART I — CANON STATUS

## 2. No Identified Canon Flag

**Status:** `RESEARCH CONCLUSION`

The reviewed Bohemia / Armaverse source material clearly establishes:

- Karzeghistan as a country;
- Mazargan as its capital in preserved AAN material;
- the Shapur / Sharig border conflict;
- Karzeghistan's relationship with Takistan and NATO forces.

However, no sufficiently authoritative official Karzeghistani:

```text
national flag
state emblem
military roundel
police badge
```

has been identified in the reviewed source set.

Therefore the project will **not** claim an existing flag is canonical unless stronger primary material is discovered later.

---

## 3. Fan Designs

Community-made Karzeghistan flags or faction interpretations are not project canon.

They may be interesting references but must not be treated as:

```text
Bohemia-established national identity
```

without evidence.

---

## 4. Project Authority

Karzeghistan's contemporary national symbols are therefore:

```text
UKSF EXTENSION
```

This gives the project freedom to create a coherent 2026 identity while preserving all established geography and political history.

# PART II — DESIGN PRINCIPLES

## 5. Country Character

The national identity should communicate:

```text
COASTAL GREEN SEA STATE
+
PERSIAN CULTURAL DIRECTION
+
FUNCTIONING REPUBLIC
+
REGIONAL INDEPENDENCE
+
MODERN CIVILIAN STATE
```

It should not visually imply:

```text
Iran
Afghanistan
Takistan
Gulf monarchy
Soviet republic
NATO member state
```

---

## 6. Contrast with Takistan

Takistan already uses a strong:

```text
WHITE / BLACK / GREEN
+
CRESCENT / CROWN / STARS
```

identity.

Karzeghistan should avoid:

- black/white/green tricolour;
- crescent as primary national device;
- crown;
- seven-star arrangement.

The countries should be immediately distinguishable on:

- shoulder flags;
- vehicle decals;
- map graphics.

# PART III — NATIONAL COLOUR SYSTEM

## 7. Locked Palette

Karzeghistan uses three primary national colours:

```text
DEEP TEAL
OFF-WHITE
WARM GOLD
```

### Deep Teal

Represents the country's Green Sea/coastal identity and forms the dominant national colour.

### Off-White

Provides a neutral civic field and high visual contrast.

### Warm Gold

Used for the national device and formal state accents.

---

## 8. Supporting Service Colours

Additional service colours may include:

```text
NAVY
DARK GREEN
CHARCOAL
SILVER
```

These are service-specific and do not replace the core national palette.

---

## 9. Exact Colour Values

Do **not** lock final RGB/HEX values until artwork testing.

Reason:

- in-game lighting;
- PAA compression;
- fabric materials;
- vehicle roughness;
- emissive/UI rendering;

all affect perceived colour.

A later art-production pass should lock master values after texture tests.

## 13. Geometry

Recommended:

- eight equal major points;
- rotational symmetry;
- no internal text;
- simple enough for a 16–32 px icon;
- suitable for stencil use.

Possible construction:

```text
two overlapping four-point geometric forms
```

or:

```text
single continuous eight-point sun form
```

The final form must be original rather than traced from a real national emblem.

## 16. No Crescent Required

The flag deliberately does not use a crescent.

It simply gives the state a civic/geographic national identity distinct from Takistan.

Do not encode Sunni/Shia or other sectarian identity into the national emblem.

The project has not defined a sectarian demographic system for Karzeghistan.

# PART VIII — NATIONAL EMBLEM / STATE DEVICE


---

## 21. Wave Lines

The wave element refers to:

- Green Sea coast;

- trade.

Keep it abstract.

- KDF uses the rosette plus a military shield/sword motif.
- KDF primary service palette is teal/gold.
- KNP uses the rosette within a police badge/shield system.
- KNP primary service palette is navy/white/silver with national accents.
- KBG uses the rosette plus border/plateau symbolism.
- KBG primary service palette is dark green/off-white with national accents.

- SPF does not use the state-service badge family.
- SPF may occasionally use the national flag but retains its own faction identity.
- Legal Sharig autonomy politics remains visually separable from SPF.
- National identity artwork is created as original vector master art.

# PART XXXVIII — CURRENT CONCLUSION

Karzeghistan now has a national visual system that is deliberately distinct from every neighbouring project faction:

```text
TAKISTAN
white / black / green
crescent / crown / stars

KARZEGHISTAN
deep teal / off-white / gold
eight-point civic rosette

SPF
Sharig regional / paramilitary identity

ARDISTAN
future identity — must remain visually separate
```

The recommended national flag is intentionally simple:

```text
TEAL
──────
   GOLD ROSETTE
──────
OFF-WHITE
```

That simplicity is useful across:

- flag cloth;
- shoulder patches;
- vehicles;
- aircraft;
- editor/UI;
- government signage.

The national rosette then provides a common visual backbone for the state:

```text
KDF
rosette + sword

KNP
rosette + police shield

KBG
rosette + border/plateau device
```

This establishes Karzeghistan as a coherent fictional country before any individual KDF/KNP/KBG equipment artwork is produced.

The next strongest faction task is the **Karzeghistani Defence Forces organisation, roles and equipment standard**, because the country now has enough political, linguistic and visual identity to define what its military actually looks like.
