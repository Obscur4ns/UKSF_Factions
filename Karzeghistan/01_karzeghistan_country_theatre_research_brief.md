# 1. Purpose

This document establishes the country-level framework for contemporary **Karzeghistan**.

It defines enough political, geographic, social and security context to support:

- the Sharig Protection Front;
- Takistan–Karzeghistan border missions;
- Karzeghistani government/security factions;
- Ardistan proxy activity;
- UK/NATO involvement;
- future Karzeghistani civilians, signage and vehicles.

The objective is not to invent an unnecessarily detailed fictional country.

The objective is to answer the questions that materially affect mission making and faction design.

# PART XXXVI — 2026 COUNTRY SUMMARY

## 98. Working Summary

> Karzeghistan is a sovereign Green Sea republic lying south of Takistan and east of Ardistan. Its coastal capital, Mazargan, remains the country's political and commercial centre. The state has a long history of tension with Takistan over the Sharig Plateau and Shapur e-Dalanper, an economically important border region that became a flashpoint during the 2012 Takistani crisis. Karzeghistan developed significant security relationships with NATO states before and during that conflict and remains Western-leaning in 2026, though it is not a NATO member and continues to balance relations with its larger neighbours. Formal relations with the post-war Takistani government have normalised, but the Sharig question remains politically sensitive. Mazargan also faces growing pressure from the Ardistan-backed Sharig Protection Front, which campaigns for greater regional autonomy and challenges government authority in parts of the borderland. Karzeghistan remains a functioning state rather than a failed one, with professional security institutions but limited resources compared with major regional powers.
