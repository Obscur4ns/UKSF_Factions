**Faction:** Takistani Border Guard (`TBG`)  
**Native project name:** `د تکستان سرحدي ځواک`  
**Operational romanisation:** `Da Takistan Sarhadi Zwak`  
**Faction type:** `SECURITY / BORDER`  
**Default side:** BLUFOR / WEST  
**Time period:** Contemporary / approximately 2026  

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the contemporary **Takistani Border Guard** as a practical `UKSF_Factions` security component.

It establishes:

- institutional role;
- relationship with TNP and TNA;
- border-post personnel;
- mobile patrol personnel;
- equipment distribution;
- weapons;
- communications;
- surveillance;
- vehicles;
- editor units;
- editor groups;
- asset requirements.

The central design rule is:

> **TBG protects and manages the border. It is not a second Army and it is not Customs.**

---

# PART I — THEATRE BASIS

## 2. Karzeghistan Border

**Status:** `CANON`

Official Arma 2 / British Armed Forces material establishes a volatile border between:

```text
Takistan
and
Karzeghistan
```

particularly around the Shapur / Sharig Plateau region.

Official material also establishes Takistani insurgent activity across that border environment.

This gives the project a strong canonical reason for a dedicated border-security institution.

---

## 3. North Takistan Relevance

**Status:** `UKSF EXTENSION`

In the 2026 North Takistan setting, the Border Guard is relevant because the wider security problem includes:

- cross-border movement;
- smuggling;
- militant facilitation;
- ordinary legal trade;
- rural border communities;
- remote terrain.

The border should not be portrayed as permanently closed.

Border security must coexist with legitimate:

- passenger movement;
- trade;
- local commerce;
- government crossings.

---

# PART II — REAL-WORLD REFERENCE PRINCIPLE

## 4. Central Asian Border Services

Contemporary Central Asian border-management programmes provide useful structural reference.

OSCE and UNODC material shows border services working on:

- protection of the state border;
- control of border crossings;
- countering illicit trafficking;
- counterterrorism;
- illegal-migration control;
- organised-crime response;
- inter-agency information sharing;
- mobile patrol;
- surveillance;
- document and cargo inspection.

This is a **broad regional reference**, not a direct copy of any one real border service.

---

## 5. Border Guard vs Customs

The project should distinguish:

### Border Guard

Responsible for:

- border security;
- patrol;
- border crossing security;
- identity/travel-document control support;
- interception of unlawful crossings;
- armed security response.

### Customs

Responsible primarily for:

- goods;
- declarations;
- tariffs;
- cargo regulation;
- commercial inspection.

They may work at the same crossing.

They are not the same service.

# PART III — ORGANISATIONAL MODEL

## 6. TBG Capability Layers

For Arma implementation, TBG uses three layers.

### Layer A — Border Post

Static crossing / checkpoint personnel.

### Layer B — Mobile Patrol

Rural and remote border patrol.

### Layer C — Border Response

Better-equipped armed response for serious border incidents.

These are project gameplay categories, not claimed canonical Takistani TO&E.

---

# PART IV — LAYER A: BORDER POST

## 7. Role

Border Post personnel represent the visible day-to-day border service.

Typical locations:

- official road crossing;
- customs compound;
- small border station;
- bridge crossing;
- government checkpoint.

---

## 8. Visual Identity

Typical:

- distinct TBG uniform or uniform accents;
- soft cap;
- TBG badge;
- radio;
- rifle or sidearm depending threat level;
- binoculars;
- official signage;
- marked pickup/SUV nearby.

---

# PART VI — LAYER C: BORDER RESPONSE

## 15. Role

Border Response is a small higher-capability element.

Purpose:

- reinforce attacked posts;
- respond to serious armed incursions;
- support interdiction of organised armed groups;
- secure a crossing during major incidents;
- hold until TNA reinforcement arrives.

---

## 16. Limits

Border Response is **not**:

- mechanised infantry;
- special operations;
- national counter-terrorism force.

If the incident develops into sustained military combat:

```text
TBG
   ↓
requests / receives
TNA support
```

TBG handles normal border-security incidents.

- sustained organised armed attack;
- military incursion;
- large insurgent concentration;
- requirement for heavy weapons.

# PART XXXIII — DESIGN RED LINES

## 94. Do Not Make TBG a Light Infantry Brigade

Avoid:

- APC platoons;
- tank units;
- routine RPG teams;
- mortars at every post;
- full military camouflage on everyone;
- universal NVGs.

---

## 95. Do Not Make TBG Unarmed Gate Staff

North Takistan's border environment is too insecure for an entirely administrative service.

Armed guards and rifle-equipped mobile patrols are appropriate.

---

## 96. Do Not Merge TBG and Customs

They are complementary government services with different functions.

---

## 97. Do Not Merge TBG and TNP

Domestic policing and border security remain separate responsibilities.
