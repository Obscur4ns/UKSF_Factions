---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the **traditional Takistani insurgent faction** for `UKSF_Factions`.

The faction exists to fill a role deliberately separate from:

- NTEF;
- Takistani National Army;
- Takistani National Police;
- Takistani Border Guard;
- criminal armed groups.

The central design objective is:

> **TNR is a locally rooted Takistani resistance movement, not a transnational extremist organisation.**

It should enable missions in which:

```text
TNA / UKSF
      vs
TNR
      vs
NTEF
```

or any other relationship configured by the mission.

# PART II — CANON FOUNDATION

## 3. Armed Rural Society

**Status:** `CANON`

Bohemia's official *Private Military Company* faction material describes Takistani society as still being based mainly around tribal structures.

It states that men in the countryside commonly possess small arms for hunting and for defending their:

- family;
- village;
- local community.

The same source says many tribes remained loyal to the royal family despite attempts by the government to weaken the royalist faction.

This provides a strong canonical basis for:

- local armed traditions;
- village defence;
- non-state armed groups;
- political loyalties independent of the central government.

---

## 4. Post-Invasion Insurgency

**Status:** `CANON`

Official *Arma 2: Reinforcements* material describes a:

```text
large-scale outbreak of guerrilla insurgency
```

in post-invasion Takistan.

British forces were deployed to reinforce coalition troops fighting that insurgency.

Therefore a broad, locally rooted Takistani insurgent tradition is already canonical.

---

## 5. Insurgency Was Later Quelled

**Status:** `CANON`

Official *Private Military Company* material states that British and coalition forces subsequently:

```text
successfully quelled the insurgent uprising
```

and that NATO's Green Sea deployment then began a strategic drawdown.

This is important.

TNR should **not** be described as one unchanged organisation continuously fighting from 2012 to 2026.

---

## 6. Original Takistan Locals

**Status:** `CANON GAME CONTENT`

Bohemia's Operation Arrowhead infantry reference includes a distinct:

```text
Takistan Locals
```

Independent/guerrilla faction.

Documented roles include:

- guerrilla with AK-pattern rifle;
- Automatic Rifleman;
- RPG-7 fighter;
- RPG ammunition carrier;
- Bonesetter;
- Warlord.

This demonstrates that the original local resistance identity was deliberately broader than a single generic rifleman.

---

## 7. Original Local Vehicles

**Status:** `CANON GAME CONTENT`

Operation Arrowhead vehicle references include:

```text
BRDM-2 [Takistan Local]
BRDM-2 (HQ) [Takistan Local]
```

This establishes precedent for local/guerrilla forces occasionally possessing captured or inherited armoured vehicles.

It does **not** mean TNR should become a mechanised faction.

# PART III — CONTEMPORARY SUCCESSOR

## 8. Historical Development

**Status:** `UKSF EXTENSION`

Recommended continuity:

```text
pre-war local armed traditions
        ↓
royalist / anti-regime local networks
        ↓
2012 post-invasion guerrilla uprising
        ↓
coalition counter-insurgency
        ↓
major insurgency suppressed
        ↓
armed networks fragment / demobilise / persist locally
        ↓
post-war political grievances continue
        ↓
new local alliances emerge
        ↓
TNR forms as a loose resistance umbrella
```

---

## 9. TNR Is Not the Old Militia Renamed

TNR is not a direct surviving command structure from 2012.

It contains:

- descendants of older local networks;
- former insurgents;
- former regime opponents;
- local armed groups;
- younger nationalist recruits;
- village defence elements;
- disaffected former government personnel;
- local political patrons.

This allows continuity without pretending nothing changed.

# PART V — LANGUAGE CONTINUITY NOTE

## 13. Project Language Decision

`UKSF_Factions` uses **Pashtu** as the contemporary Takistani language convention.

An older Bohemia Operation Arrowhead Q&A described Takistani speech as Persian.

The project therefore treats Pashtu as a **deliberate UKSF continuity choice**, not as a claim that Bohemia originally established Pashtu.

Existing Armaverse and terrain Latin place names remain unchanged.

## 17. What TNR Wants

There is no single detailed manifesto.

Common themes may include:

- stronger local autonomy;
- protection of local communities;
- resistance to outside political control;
- opposition to foreign military influence;
- opposition to NTEF;
- preservation of traditional local authority;
- political reform of the post-war state.

Different components may emphasise different goals.

---

## 18. Internal Diversity

TNR is an umbrella rather than a perfectly unified party.

Potential currents:

### Nationalist

Prioritises Takistani sovereignty and state reform.

### Localist

Primarily defends a district, valley, village network or local power structure.

### Royalist

Retains sympathy for historic royalist traditions.

### Republican / old resistance

Descended politically from earlier anti-regime or post-war armed movements.

### Pragmatist

Less ideological; participates because of local grievance, patronage or security concerns.

---

## 19. No Single Theology

TNR operates in a culturally Islamic society but should not be defined primarily by religious extremism.

Religion may influence:

- personal identity;
- local legitimacy;
- community life.

It should not make TNR interchangeable with NTEF.

# PART X — TNR VS UKSF / NATO

## 27. Baseline

TNR is suspicious of or hostile toward foreign military presence.

# PART XLIV — CURRENT CONCLUSION

The traditional insurgent faction should not be:

```text
ANOTHER EXTREMIST GROUP
```

It should represent a surviving and evolving tradition of:

```text
LOCAL ARMED AUTONOMY
+
TAKISTANI NATIONALISM
+
POST-WAR GRIEVANCE
+
HISTORICAL RESISTANCE NETWORKS
```

The resulting North Takistan conflict becomes:

```text
TAKISTANI STATE
      │
      ├──────── hostile / contested ──────── TNR
      │                                       │
      │                                       │ hostile
      │                                       ▼
      └──────────── hostile ──────────────── NTEF
```

with UKSF able to encounter TNR as:

- enemy;
- negotiating partner;
- temporary ally;
- intelligence source;
- reconciliation target.

That political flexibility is TNR's defining gameplay value and is the clearest way to keep it distinct from NTEF.
