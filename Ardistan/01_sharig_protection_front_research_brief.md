# UKSF_Factions — Faction Research Brief 05
## Sharig Protection Front (SPF)

**Project:** `UKSF_Factions`  
**Author:** `UKSF Surplus`  
**Document:** Faction Research Brief 05  
**Status:** Research / Pre-production  
**Created:** 2026-08-09  
**Faction:** Sharig Protection Front  
**Working acronym:** `SPF`  
**Faction type:** `PROXY / PARAMILITARY / REGIONAL`  
**Primary sponsor:** Ardistan  
**Primary operating region:** Sharig Plateau / Takistan–Karzeghistan border region  
**Default side:** OPFOR / EAST — provisional  
**Time period:** Contemporary / approximately 2026  

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines the first **foreign-backed regional proxy faction** for `UKSF_Factions`.

The faction exists to add a form of armed actor that is deliberately different from:

- NTEF;
- TNR;
- TNA;
- ordinary criminal militias;
- conventional foreign armies.

The central concept is:

> **A locally rooted border movement whose capability has been materially increased by covert or semi-covert support from Ardistan.**

The proxy must have:

- its own local political agenda;
- its own leadership;
- its own recruitment base;
- its own faction identity;

while receiving meaningful external:

- funding;
- equipment;
- training;
- communications;
- intelligence or advisory support.

---

# PART I — ARMAVERSE FOUNDATION

## 2. Karzeghistan

**Status:** `CANON`

Karzeghistan is an established Armaverse country bordering Takistan.

Official British Armed Forces material places part of the expansion's fighting across the border in Karzeghistan.

The Shapur / Sharig Plateau area is described as particularly volatile.

---

## 3. Sharig Plateau

**Status:** `CANON`

Official Bohemia material identifies the:

```text
Sharig Plateau
```

as part of the Takistan–Karzeghistan border dispute/security environment.

Shapur is located within this broader context.

The area has experienced:

- armed patrols;
- insurgent activity;
- cross-border violence;
- international concern.

This makes Sharig one of the strongest existing Armaverse locations for a future regional proxy conflict.

---

## 4. Karzeghistan Is Not Takistan

The faction should not treat Karzeghistan as simply:

```text
East Takistan
```

It is a separate country with its own political interests.

The exact 2026 Karzeghistani political system remains deliberately undefined.

---

# PART II — ARDISTAN

## 5. Ardistan Exists

**Status:** `CANON / ARMAVERSE GEOGRAPHY`

Ardistan is an established Armaverse country.

Its appearance in historical Armaverse mapping/reference material is reinforced by later Arma 3 material referring to:

```text
Eastern Ardistan
```

However, Bohemia provides little detailed contemporary political or military lore.

---

## 6. Why Ardistan Is Useful

This lack of detailed canon is an advantage for `UKSF_Factions`.

Ardistan can be developed as a meaningful regional actor without contradicting:

- an established government type;
- detailed existing armed forces;
- existing alliances;
- established modern foreign policy.

---

# PART III — 2026 ARDISTAN

## 7. Project Interpretation

**Status:** `UKSF EXTENSION`

In the 2026 UKSF continuity, Ardistan is a significant regional state with greater conventional and institutional capacity than the non-state armed groups operating around northern Takistan.

It seeks influence across the wider region through a mixture of:

- diplomacy;
- trade;
- security relationships;
- political patronage;
- intelligence activity;
- indirect support to selected armed partners.

---

## 8. Do Not Make Ardistan "Iran"

Ardistan should not be a one-to-one fictional copy of Iran.

Avoid automatically importing:

- Iranian flags;
- IRGC terminology;
- real Iranian ranks;
- Iranian political institutions;
- real proxy organisations;
- real religious-political structures.

Real-world state-proxy relationships may inform the broad design.

The resulting state remains Armaverse-fictional.

---

# PART IV — SPONSOR MOTIVES

## 9. Ardistan's Strategic Interest

**Status:** `UKSF EXTENSION`

Ardistan supports SPF because influence over the Sharig border region provides strategic leverage.

Potential interests include:

- political influence in Karzeghistan;
- influence over Takistani border affairs;
- limiting Western/UK influence;
- securing commercial access;
- shaping future regional settlements;
- access to local political networks;
- creating strategic depth without deploying regular forces.

---

## 10. Deniability

Ardistan benefits when its involvement remains politically ambiguous.

The relationship should therefore not normally look like:

```text
Ardistan Army
wearing SPF patch
```

Instead support appears through:

- equipment;
- finance;
- training;
- advisers;
- communications;
- intelligence;
- logistics.

---

## 11. Deniability Is Not Absolute

The project should not make Ardistan's involvement a ridiculous secret that everybody somehow fails to notice.

Preferred situation:

> Ardistan publicly denies directing SPF while regional governments and foreign intelligence services widely suspect or assess substantial Ardistan support.

This is more useful for mission writing.

---

# PART V — PROXY AUTONOMY

## 12. SPF Is Not Ardistan's Remote-Control Army

**Decision:** `LOCKED`

SPF has its own interests.

Ardistan can:

- influence;
- fund;
- equip;
- pressure;

but cannot perfectly dictate every local decision.

---

## 13. Sponsor–Proxy Friction

Possible disagreements:

- Ardistan wants de-escalation while SPF wants retaliation;
- Ardistan wants political leverage while SPF seeks territory;
- SPF commanders divert equipment to local priorities;
- Ardistan favours one SPF commander over another;
- SPF negotiates locally without sponsor approval.

This creates useful campaign depth.

# PART VI — WORKING FACTION NAME

## 14. English / NATO Designation

# Sharig Protection Front

Acronym:

```text

```

---

## 15. Why "Sharig"

The name ties the faction to an established Armaverse geographic feature.

It communicates that the faction is:

- regional;
- territorial;
- locally rooted.

This distinguishes it from NTEF's wider extremist aspirations.

---

## 16. Why "Protection Front"

The faction publicly claims to protect:

- Sharig communities;
- local political rights;
- border populations;
- regional autonomy/security.

Whether those claims reflect actual conduct is mission-dependent.

---

## 17. Native Name

**Decision:** `DEFER`

Karzeghistan's language convention has not yet been established.

Do not give SPF a Pashtu native name solely because Takistan uses Pashtu in this project.

SPF may contain Pashtu-speaking Takistani members, but its primary regional identity extends into Karzeghistan.

The native/self-designation should be created only after Karzeghistan language/cultural research.

- former TNR members;

- advisers;
- trainers;
- intelligence personnel;
- specialist technicians.

## 30. Side Constraint

With:

```text
TNA/UKSF = BLUFOR
NTEF = OPFOR
TNR = INDEPENDENT
```

there is no unused normal combat side for SPF.

### Provisional choice

```text
SPF = OPFOR
```

This makes SPF immediately hostile to:

- TNA;
- UKSF;
- TNR where INDEPENDENT is configured hostile to OPFOR.

But it means SPF and NTEF will be friendly at engine side level.

---

## 31. Interpretation of Shared OPFOR Side

Shared OPFOR does **not** mean SPF and NTEF are ideological allies.

For normal faction design, treat this as an engine/editor compromise.

Campaigns requiring SPF–NTEF combat may need:

- mission-specific scripting;
- side reassignment;
- unit substitution;
- another mission-architecture solution.

Do not duplicate the entire faction onto another side in the core addon solely to solve every possible relationship.
